Typography is undoubtedly one of the most critical skills that every designer should possess.

From selecting the right font to ensuring that the typography is easy to read and visually appealing, typography can make or break a design.

However, it is also essential to note that you don't need extensive typography skills to find a good body font.

While a deep understanding of typography can undoubtedly help, several online tools like [paratype](https://www.paratype.com/) can help you identify suitable body fonts.

Additionally, it's crucial to remember that readability and legibility are key factors when choosing a body font.
